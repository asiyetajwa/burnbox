# Burn Box brand, the short version

**Name:** Burn Box. Two words, both capitalised. The repo slug `burnbox` is the only exception.

**Idea:** a calm box, loud gear. The box is a quiet, machined stack of pastel compartments on studio grey. The gear is colour-blocked, stickered safety equipment. The product looks calm; colour is saved for what the sample tried to do.

**Colour:** containment pastels (Sage, Deep Sage, Mint Glass, Blush, Butter, Clay, Chrome) for the product. Blast brights (Signal Red, Hot Pink, Burn Orange, Ultraviolet, Sticker Lime, Sky) only for evidence and stickers, under ten percent of any frame. Verdicts: Safe is Deep Sage, Suspicious is Butter, Malicious is Signal Red. Values in `tokens.css`.

**Type:** Bricolage Grotesque 800, condensed, for headlines, verdicts and stickers. Instrument Sans for body. IBM Plex Mono for traces, run codes, receipts and UTC timestamps.

**Materials:** chrome sphere is the sample; a grid of white spheres is the canaries (one turns hot pink when touched); frosted glass is the sandbox wall; an open frame is the drop zone.

**Stickers:** Super Safe, Contained, Canary, Burned stamp, Blast Radius 0 tape. One or two per screen, slightly rotated, cream die-cut edge, each marking a state.

**Never:** fire, skulls, hazard tape, hacker green, glows, gradients on colour blocks, em dashes, diagonal arrows on links.

The reference images (a pastel modular cube render and a stack of colour-blocked helmets) are not committed; they are third-party work used only as direction.
